# 01 — Product Overview

## Purpose
Give one child a fun, low-pressure way to practice what they're learning in school, in short daily sessions, while letting a parent quickly add this week's material (e.g. Friday's spelling list) and see progress.

## Users
| User | Age / role | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Child (primary) | 5–11, early or developing reader | Big tap targets, minimal reading to navigate, read-aloud, encouragement, short sessions |
| Parent (secondary) | Adult | Fast deck creation, control over difficulty and time, simple progress view, no ads or data sharing |

v1 supports **one parent and up to 4 child profiles** on one device (siblings).

## Goals
- **PO-01** Child can start practicing in ≤ 2 taps from opening the app.
- **PO-02** Parent can enter a 15-word spelling list in under 2 minutes.
- **PO-03** Sessions default to 5–10 minutes (configurable).
- **PO-04** Cards the child struggles with appear more often; mastered cards less often.
- **PO-05** Works fully offline after first load.
- **PO-06** No ads, no in-app purchases, no third-party tracking.

## In scope (v1)
- Subjects: spelling (read aloud → child spells), addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, fractions (compare, reduce, improper ↔ mixed/whole), geography (U.S. states & capitals, continents, oceans), sight words, plus custom decks.
- Practice modes: flip-and-self-check, type-the-answer, multiple choice, number pad, fraction entry, spelling (hear it, type it), spelling test day.
- Two child profiles at setup (grade 3), per `08-open-decisions.md`.
- Auto-generated math fact decks.
- Rewards (stars, streaks, stickers), progress tracking, parent dashboard.
- Read-aloud (text-to-speech) for prompts.

## Out of scope (v1)
- Accounts, cloud sync, multi-device sharing (see `08-open-decisions.md`)
- Teacher/classroom features
- Multiplayer or social features
- AI-generated content inside the app
- Handwriting recognition

## Success criteria
- Child chooses to use it 4+ days a week for a month without being prompted every time.
- Measurable accuracy improvement on weekly spelling lists and targeted math facts.
- Parent spends < 5 minutes a week on setup.
